# Returns Policy

Eligible products may be returned according to the product-specific return conditions. Products must generally be unused, undamaged, and returned with their original packaging where applicable.

Perishable, opened, or hygiene-sensitive products may not be eligible for return unless the item is damaged, defective, incorrect, or otherwise covered by the applicable policy.

Customers should contact support as soon as possible when they receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product.
